# CIVITA Contributors

CIVITA is developed collaboratively within the Grand Challenge on the Calibration, Validation, and Benchmarking of Concrete Computational Models.

## Copyright ownership

Copyright in individual software contributions remains with the applicable rights holders. The initial contributors are recognized for their respective original work. A repository or source file may have distinct copyright holders and notices. The collective attribution convention leaves those rights and notices in place.

## Future contributors

Contributors and maintainers may propose additions to this file through a pull request. Record the contributor’s preferred name and, where supplied, their affiliation at the time of the contribution. Use Git history as supplementary attribution evidence. Determine copyright ownership from the relevant rights and circumstances, including any institutional or contractual terms, and preserve individual source notices. The collective copyright notice stays the same as contributors are added.

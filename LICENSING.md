# CIVITA Licensing Policy

This policy applies to the repositories in the [CIVITA GitHub organization](https://github.com/civita-network). CIVITA is developed within the Grand Challenge on the Calibration, Validation, and Benchmarking of Concrete Computational Models. Its software and scientific materials support reproducible research and reuse in academic and commercial settings.

| Content | Applicable license or terms |
| --- | --- |
| Original CIVITA software, services, libraries, and supporting scripts | [MIT](https://opensource.org/license/mit) |
| Original CIVITA JSON Schemas and software-facing interface specifications | MIT, subject to rights verification and any specific exception |
| Authorized CIVITA-authored scientific documentation, benchmark specifications, and scientific protocols | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode) |
| Eligible CIVITA-owned public metadata and controlled vocabularies | [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode) |
| Original experimental datasets and submitted computational results | The license for each dataset or submission. CC BY 4.0 is preferred when the rights holder authorizes it. |
| Legacy datasets, imported metadata, and other third-party material | The original applicable rights and permissions |
| CIVITA name, logo, and project identity | Trademark rights are outside these license grants |

A repository’s root license covers its principal eligible content. Its README, local licensing notices, and item-specific rights statements identify the scope and exceptions. Preserve existing notices and attributions when redistributing material. Refer uncertain ownership or conflicting terms to the maintainer for review before making a new grant.

## Software and ownership

CIVITA uses `Copyright (c) 2026-present CIVITA contributors` as collective attribution. Copyright in original software contributions stays with the respective contributors or other applicable rights holders. The attribution convention carries no transfer of ownership to CIVITA or the Grand Challenge Working Group. Preserve existing individual and third-party copyright notices. MIT permits commercial reuse and integration with proprietary software subject to its standard conditions, including preservation of the copyright and permission notices.

## Scientific material and data

Apply CC BY 4.0 only to material authorized by the rights holder. Preserve the applicable license and provenance for each dataset, including legacy datasets. Record authorization before offering a new license. Distinguish reuse of factual observations from reproduction of protected text, tables, figures, photographs, and other source material.

The license on submitted computational results covers those results. Rights to the underlying model, numerical implementation, commercial framework, and input material remain governed by their own terms. Submission of results carries no obligation to release those implementations.

CC0 applies only to eligible metadata and vocabularies whose rights holders can dedicate them. Preserve the provenance and terms of imported metadata and referenced standards. Cite CIVITA and the relevant release in scientific work even where CC0 imposes no attribution requirement.

Future scientific objects should carry machine-readable rights information at the relevant dataset, submission, or asset level. Candidate fields are `license`, `rights_holder`, `source`, `attribution`, `license_url`, and `reuse_conditions`. Record the upstream source and version alongside imported material.

## Project identity

The content and software licenses carry no independent grant of trademark rights in the CIVITA name, logo, or project identity. Describe derivatives and integrations without implying official CIVITA endorsement.

The [central repository’s CC BY 4.0 license](LICENSE) applies to its original project and governance documentation. See [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md) for recognition and ownership, and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for contribution terms.

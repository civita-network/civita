# Contributing to CIVITA

CIVITA welcomes academic researchers, research laboratories, graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, software developers, engineering practitioners, and industrial organizations.

## Software contributions

> By submitting an original software contribution to CIVITA, contributors agree that their contribution may be distributed under the MIT License. Contributors retain copyright ownership of their respective contributions, subject to any applicable pre-existing rights, and confirm that they have the authority to license the submitted material under these terms.

Contributors are responsible for applicable institutional and contractual obligations. Identify third-party code, verify authority to include it, and preserve its license and required notices. Eligible software contributions are accepted under the license grant above, with copyright retained by the applicable rights holders. Copyright assignment is unnecessary.

## Pull requests

1. Fork the target repository or create an authorized development branch.
2. Develop the contribution and document how it was checked.
3. Open a pull request against the target repository.
4. Complete the licensing confirmation checklist and identify any third-party material.
5. Participate in technical review.
6. Address requested changes.
7. An appropriate maintainer merges the contribution after approval.

## Scientific contributions

Identify the applicable rights, provenance, and reuse terms for datasets, benchmark specifications, results, documentation, and any imported material. CC BY 4.0 is preferred for original datasets and results when the rights holder authorizes it. Use the identified dataset or submission license. The terms for scientific results apply to those results. Rights over software or models used to generate them remain governed by their own licenses or agreements.

See the [licensing policy](LICENSING.md) for content-specific terms and [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md) for contributor recognition.
